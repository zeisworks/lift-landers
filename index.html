Every effort is rewarded.

Fair questions.

Do I have to be a member before I start training?
No. Start with the free day pass and tour — and every training pack includes a gym membership (one to nine months depending on the pack), so you're never paying twice.
Am I signing a contract?
Gym memberships are month-to-month with no contracts. Training is prepaid in the pack you choose — when it's done, it's done, and you decide what's next.
I've never really lifted. Is this place for me?
Yes — that's exactly what the evaluation is for. New gyms are intimidating; we want you to know you belong here. Your plan starts where you are, and your coach is with you for every rep.
When can I actually train?
Staffed hours run seven days a week (Mon–Thu 6 AM–7 PM, Fri 6 AM–5 PM, Sat 6 AM–2 PM, Sun 8 AM–2 PM), and members get 24/7 keycard access — so your sessions fit your schedule, not ours.
What types of training do you offer?
One-on-one personal training, sports performance training for athletes, weight-loss and body-composition programs, small-group training (2–4 people), and online training programs you can follow from anywhere. Every option starts from the same evaluation.
How am I matched with a trainer?
After your evaluation, you're paired with the certified trainer best suited to your goals, experience level, and schedule — and your program is built around what the evaluation found, not a template.
What if I need to cancel a session?
Give 24 hours' notice and you keep the session. Cancel inside 24 hours (or no-show) and the session is forfeited. If your trainer cancels, you're rescheduled at no penalty — and if it can't be rescheduled, you get an extra session.
Do training sessions expire?
No — sessions never expire. The gym membership included with your pack runs on its own timeframe (1–9 months depending on pack size). Packages are non-refundable and can't be transferred to another person.
What does it cost, really?
It's all published above: a one-time $100 evaluation, then $85–$100 per session depending on pack size — gym membership included. Training is sold in full packs only (no single sessions). The day pass and tour are free. No initiation fee, no quote appointment.
