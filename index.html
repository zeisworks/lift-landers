Every effort is rewarded.

Fair questions.

Do I have to be a member before I start training?
No. Start with the free day pass and tour — and every training pack includes a gym membership (one to nine months depending on the pack), so you're never paying twice.
Am I signing a contract?
Gym memberships are month-to-month with no contracts. Training is prepaid in the pack you choose — when it's done, it's done, and you decide what's next.
I've never really lifted. Is this place for me?
Yes — that's exactly what the evaluation is for. New gyms are intimidating; we want you to know you belong here. Your plan starts where you are, and your coach is with you for every rep.
When can I actually train?
Staffed hours run seven days a week (Mon–Thu 6 AM–7 PM, Fri 6 AM–5 PM, Sat 6 AM–2 PM, Sun 8 AM–2 PM), and members get 24/7 keycard access — so your sessions fit your schedule, not ours.
What does it cost, really?
It's all published above: a one-time $100 evaluation, then $85–$100 per session depending on pack size — gym membership included. The day pass and tour are free. No initiation fee, no quote appointment.
